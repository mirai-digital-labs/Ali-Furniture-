Our Story

Craftsmanship & Trust by Malik Irfan

Ali Furniture & Deco Point, led by Malik Irfan, is your premier destination on Farooq Azam Road, near Niazi Chowk, Jhang Saddar. We craft every article with pure passion, employing seasoned artisans who master the art of solid wood fabrication.

From royal wedding sets and luxury velvet sofas to executive office desks and wardrobes, we use 100% genuine Sheesham (Tali) and Ash wood. With our Easy Exchange Policy and nationwide delivery, your satisfaction is our lifelong promise.

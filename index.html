Our Story

Three Generations of Woodworking Excellence

Ali Furniture began in 2009 as a small family workshop in Lahore. Today we are a full showroom and manufacturing unit — but every piece is still built the same way: by hand, by craftsmen who have spent their lives with wood.

We source solid Sheesham (Tali) and Ash wood, season it properly, and finish it with hand-rubbed oils. No particle board. No shortcuts. Just furniture that your grandchildren will argue over.
